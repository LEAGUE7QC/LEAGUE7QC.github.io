The feedback throughout the season has been fantastic and will continue to be essential in shaping the league going forward.

I have complete confidence that whoever succeeds me will continue to uphold the integrity of the league while making the necessary improvements to help it keep growing.

Thank you all again for an unforgettable Season 4.
